Q: How do I aim it?
To aim your ChatrBox effectively, follow these steps: 1. **Understand the Motion Sensor**: The ChatrBox has a cone-shaped motion sensor at the front that can detect movement up to 15 feet away. 2. **Use Motion Detection Shields**: You have four shields to adjust the detection area: - **Narrow View**: For precise targeting. - **Threshold View**: For detecting crossing motion. - **Side View**: Blocks sides, ideal for use against walls or doors. - **Corner View**: Detects motion from side angles. 3. **Placement**: For smaller areas, position the unit closer to the target area. Remember, the detection cone narrows with distance, so accurate placement is crucial for successful training. 🐾 You're paws-itively on the right track! If you need more guidance, feel free to ask.
