Q: How do I power on Chatrbox?
To power on your ChatrBox, follow these steps: 1. **Install Power Source:** - Insert 4 high-quality AAA batteries into the device, ensuring they are properly oriented according to the markings in the battery compartment. - Alternatively, you can use a USB-C power adapter (5V) to power the device. 2. **Power On:** - Press and hold the top button until you hear "Power On" and see a pulsing blue light. This indicates that the device is in Pause Mode. 3. **Begin Motion Detection:** - To start the motion detection, press the power button once more. You'll hear "Unpaused" and see a white light flash faster over 10 seconds, indicating the device is actively monitoring for movement. If you encounter any issues with powering on, make sure the batteries are fresh and properly installed, or try using the USB-C adapter.
