Q: How do I power on Chatrbox?
To power on your ChatrBox: 1. **Install Power Source:** - Insert 4 high-quality AAA batteries or connect a USB-C power adapter (5V). Avoid using rechargeable batteries for optimal performance. 2. **Power On:** - Hold the top button until you hear "Power On" and see a pulsing blue light. This indicates that the device is in Pause Mode. 3. **Activate Motion Detection:** - To begin motion detection, press the power button once more. You'll hear "Unpaused" and see a white light flash faster over 10 seconds. The ChatrBox will then actively monitor for movement. 🐾 Tail wags and good vibes!
