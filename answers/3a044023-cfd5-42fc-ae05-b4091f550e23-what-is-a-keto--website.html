Q: What is a keto website?
A keto website typically provides information, recipes, and resources related to the ketogenic diet. This diet focuses on high-fat, low-carbohydrate intake to promote ketosis, a metabolic state where the body burns fat for fuel instead of carbohydrates. Such websites often include meal plans, nutritional tips, and success stories to support individuals following the keto lifestyle.
