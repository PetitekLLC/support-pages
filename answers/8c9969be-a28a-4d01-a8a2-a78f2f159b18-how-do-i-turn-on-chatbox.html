Q: How do I turn on Chatbox?

It's recommended to use premium alkaline batteries like Duracell or Energizer for optimal performance. 2. **Power On**: Press and hold the top button until you hear "Power On" and see a pulsing blue light. This indicates that the device is in Pause Mode. 3. **Activate Motion Detection**: To begin motion detection, press the power button once more.
