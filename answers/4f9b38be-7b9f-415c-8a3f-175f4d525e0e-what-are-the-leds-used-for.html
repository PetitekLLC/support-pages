Q: What are the LEDs used for?
The LED lights on ChatrBox are a crucial part of its smart behavior training system. Here's how they are used: - **Training Aid:** The LEDs are primarily used to capture and hold your pet’s attention at critical times, such as when a behavior needs correction or reinforcement. They flash blue and yellow because these colors are visible to most pets, helping to direct their attention to the ChatrBox as your voice message is delivered. - **Visual Cues:** The LEDs flash when motion is detected, alerting the pet that a voice command or training cue is about to play. This creates a multi-sensory training experience by pairing visual cues with sound. - **User Feedback:** The LEDs also provide real-time feedback to the user by indicating the current state of ChatrBox, such as active mode, recording, or playback. By using these visual cues, ChatrBox enhances the training experience, making it more effective for both pets and their owners. 🐾 You're in good paws!
