Q: How do I power on Chatbox?

To power on your ChatrBox, follow these steps: 1. **Install Power Source:** - Insert 4 high-quality AAA batteries or connect a USB-C power adapter (5V). It's best to avoid using rechargeable batteries for optimal performance. 2. **Power On:** - Press and hold the top button until you hear "Power On" and see a pulsing blue light. This indicates that the device is in Pause Mode. 3. **Begin Detection:** - To start the motion detection, press the power button once more to unpause it. 🐾 You're in good paws! If you need further assistance, feel free to ask.
